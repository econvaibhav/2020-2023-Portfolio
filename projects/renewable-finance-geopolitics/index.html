Climate & political economy

Following renewable-energy finance

Who finances renewable projects, and how are those flows organised?

Open the work ↓Data assembly Visualisation Comparative analysis

01 / Substance

The question behind the work

The paper asks how the geography and form of renewable-energy finance relate to development. It brings together funding instruments, recipient income groups and regional comparisons, with a focus on India, China and African countries.

02 / Methods

What can disaggregating loans, grants and equity reveal?

IRENA investment data, World Bank income classifications and GDP measures; data wrangling, maps, grouped tables and comparisons of loans, grants and equity. The unit of comparison and the financing instrument are kept visible.

Read for the argument

What the work brings out

The analysis decomposes financing into loans, grants and equity and examines where funds originate and where projects are located. It also explicitly leaves the proposed connection between the observed patterns and geopolitical motives as a question requiring further work.

Read it in the original · page 12 ↗

The connection

The strongest methodological move is to separate “investment” into types of funding and relationships between lenders and recipients. Describing these flows is a different task from establishing the motives behind them.

From question to evidence

How the work is put together

01Combine financing records with country and income-group information.
02Distinguish loans, grants and equity instead of pooling every instrument.
03Map the origin and destination of the recorded financing.
04Compare regions and income groups before interpreting the patterns.

Scope, interpretation & what is available

The source paper’s geopolitical interpretations are arguments, not demonstrated motives. The portfolio presents the data workflow without endorsing those interpretations. The paper is dated 29 September 2023 and uses the historical data identified in its references; it is not a current financing assessment.

# India & China: development comparisons

[← All projects](../../README.md) · [Project page](index.html)

A visual timeline of ideas and events in India and China, accompanied by two comparative data assignments. The assignments examine life expectancy, fertility, schooling, sectoral employment and output, using time-series charts and regressions.

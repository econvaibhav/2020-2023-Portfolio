Technology & institutions

Carbon capture, beyond the technology

What would a technological proposal need to work in a specific setting?

Open the work ↓Evidence synthesis Comparative analysis Visualisation

01 / Substance

The question behind the work

The report and presentation examine carbon capture, utilisation and storage in the context of Indian industry. They place the technical processes beside costs, environmental questions, social implications and institutional conditions.

02 / Methods

How can engineering, environmental and economic evidence be read together?

Interdisciplinary literature synthesis, technology comparison, emissions graphics and case studies. The project links a substantial written review to a designed presentation rather than treating the slides as a replacement for the underlying work.

Read for the argument

What the work brings out

The review distinguishes a possible technical pathway from the conditions needed to apply it in India. Its concluding discussion brings costs, research needs and environmental and social considerations back into the same assessment instead of treating capture technology alone as the answer.

Read it in the original · page 29 ↗

The connection

The analytical move is to distinguish technical possibility from contextual feasibility. A technology has to be understood as a system with costs, risks and institutions, not only as a diagram of a process.

## Substance

This data story explores a reported pattern in Rajasthan using comparative indicators, maps and an extended narrative. It is included as a piece of visual and interpretive work, with the question of measurement kept separate from causal explanation.

## Methods

Comparative tables, mapping, visual storytelling and literature-based interpretation. The document combines several forms of evidence rather than relying on one figure to carry the entire explanation.

## What the work brings out

The paper moves from a reported pattern to possible social explanations. Its place in the portfolio is as a worked example of visual argument-building, with an important distinction preserved: a proposed explanation in a narrative is not a demonstrated individual-level mechanism.

## The connection

The useful connection is between an intriguing pattern and the discipline of interpreting it. A narrative may suggest explanations; aggregate comparisons alone do not establish why people experience an outcome.
